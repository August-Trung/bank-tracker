Privacy Policy

BankTracker - Smart Transaction Management

🔒 Data Protected Locally

BankTracker is an application that helps you automatically track bank transactions by reading notifications on your device. All data is stored and processed completely locally on your device, ensuring absolute privacy and security.

1. App Permissions

  • Notification Access: Only used to detect transaction notifications from banking apps that you have enabled for monitoring
  • Sound & Vibration: To notify you when new transactions are recorded
  • No Internet Connection: The app does not collect or send any data to external servers by default

2. Scope of Operation

  • Only reads notifications from predefined banking apps in the system configuration
  • If you enable the "Allow analysis of unknown banks" option, the app may read notifications from other apps
  • Transaction data is 100% locally stored in a Room database on your device
  • No server connections unless you actively enable cloud backup features (if available)

3. Your Control

  • Disable Monitoring: Go to Settings → App Permissions → Notification Access and turn off permission for BankTracker
  • Export/Import Data: You can export all data to a file for backup or transfer to another device
  • Delete Data: Delete all transaction history at any time in the app's Settings section
